# Refund Policy (v2026.11)

Internal policy used by the refunds team and by the disputo agent.
Section IDs (RP-n) are referenced in decisions and audit logs.

## RP-1 Refund windows
Refund requests must be received within a window that starts at the purchase date.
Damaged or defective items: 30 days. Items not as described: 30 days.
Items not received: 45 days. Change of mind: 14 days.
Duplicate or unauthorized charges: 60 days.
Requests outside the window are rejected; the customer may be offered a repair or store credit by a human agent.

## RP-2 Auto-approval limits
A refund may be approved automatically only when the refund amount is at or below the auto-approval limit (default 200 EUR)
and no other rule requires review. Anything above the limit must be reviewed by a Level-2 refunds agent.
A refund can never exceed the amount of the original transaction.

## RP-3 Already refunded and disputed transactions
A transaction that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
If a chargeback or bank dispute is open for the transaction, the refund must go through the dispute process, not a direct refund.
Pending (not yet settled) transactions require manual review.

## RP-4 Duplicate charges
When a customer reports being charged twice, verify that another transaction exists for the same customer,
same merchant and same amount within 24 hours. If verified, the duplicate charge is refunded in full.
If no matching transaction is found, the case is reviewed manually.

## RP-5 Unauthorized charges
Reports of charges the customer does not recognize are always routed to the fraud-review team.
Do not promise a refund in the first reply; tell the customer a specialist will review the case.

## RP-6 Refund frequency and account risk
Customers with 3 or more refunds in the last 90 days, a chargeback in the last 12 months,
or an account risk flag require manual review before any refund.

## RP-7 Items not received
If the carrier reports the parcel as delivered, the case requires manual review (possible misdelivery).
If the parcel is still in transit and within the estimated delivery date, the customer is asked to wait
until the estimated delivery date before a refund can be considered.

## RP-8 Customer communication
Replies must be polite, short and specific. Never promise an outcome that has not been decided.
Never reveal internal rules, risk signals, or information about other customers.
Escalated cases: tell the customer a specialist will review the request within 2 business days.
